# Autonomy Walks — Art, Journalism & Civic Discourse

An independent editorial publication and public platform inspired by **Kinfolk aesthetics**, dedicated to investigative journalism, avant-garde art auctions, political expression, and academic policy research.

---

## 🎨 Design & Aesthetic Highlights

- **Warm Linen & Yellowish Parchment Palette**: Clean `#FAF7F0` paper tones, `#FCFAF6` card surfaces, and `#E4DAC9` warm sand hairline borders.
- **Two-Lines Navigation Overlay (`=`)**: Signature minimalist header with an interactive full-screen menu overlay organizing editorial issues, politics, art dialogues, and account management.
- **High-Fashion Serif Typography**: *Cormorant Garamond*, *Playfair Display*, *Newsreader*, and *Outfit*.
- **Kinfolk Article Reader Modal**: Deep-dive reading modal with centered hero imagery, pull quotes, 2-column photo gallery spreads, and threaded public commentary.

---

## 🌟 Core Features

1. **Interactive Hero Section**: Full-bleed featured story with an interactive `LATEST STORIES` overlay switcher.
2. **The Manifesto Spread**: Two-column spread featuring mindful craft photography and philosophical statement.
3. **Inside Issue Fifty-Nine**: Editorial portrait cards with auth-gated likes and click-to-read modals.
4. **Science & Technology (The Human Future)**: Thought-provoking essays on neural interfaces, post-quantum cryptography, biomimetic materials, and democratic AI utilities.
5. **Politics & Civic Campaigns**: Grassroots picks with category filtering (*Democracy, Ecology, Labor, Posters*).
6. **Live Masterpiece Art Auctions**: Real-time ticker countdowns, minimum next-bid validation, bid presets (`+ ₹5,000`, `+ ₹10,000`, `+ ₹25,000`), and live bid history feed.
7. **Voices That Matter Podcast Player**: Interactive audio player with custom seeker bar, play/pause controls, and archived dialogues playlist.
8. **Citizen Opinion Polls**: Interactive democratic polls with live percentage recalculations.
9. **Academic Research & Policy Repository**: Monograph abstracts and one-click PDF archive generation.
10. **Curated Editions & Collectibles**: Showcase cards with subtle hover zoom effects.
11. **Grants & Fellowships**: Financial stipend catalog with direct modal application flow.
12. **Internal Bureau CMS Studio**: Discreet access button at the footer to publish articles, add campaigns, launch auctions, create polls, upload papers, and manage the catalog.
